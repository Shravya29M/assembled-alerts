Reset: Replay history cleared; current rule configurations and versions are kept.

Rules

Edits apply from the current simulated clock onward. Duration timers are recomputed from current state. Past notifications are never changed.

Incidents

An incident is one rule watching one subject. Disabled: the rule stopped watching it (rule disabled or subject out of scope), so it closed silently, with no notification. Duration rules have no severity.

Pending timers

A duration alert waits for its deadline. In this replay a timer fires only when a later line arrives (or at end of input).

Notifications

Effective is when the condition became true. Emitted is when the replay could first know it. All times are UTC. Rows added by the last Step are highlighted.

Amber Emitted time: reported later than it became true. Duration alerts fire only when the next line arrives (or at end of input), so they are reported after their deadline.

Diagnostics

Data-quality findings: duplicate and stale lines, missing values and contradictions. They never change an alert on their own.
